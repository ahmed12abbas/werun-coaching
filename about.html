About WE RUN

We're building a running culture that lasts — getting more people moving across Riyadh and the Kingdom, with a clear focus on empowering women and developing Saudi running talent.

We train with structured, standardised programmes and guidance from regional experts, on a platform open to runners of every level. We push every member toward personal and athletic goals they can measure, and we're building a group that represents Saudi Arabia at national and international events.
